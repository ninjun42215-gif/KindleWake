# World Expansion: Dungeons, Strongholds and Settlements

**Status:** authored expansion catalogue; data is loaded by GameData, but entries do not become playable maps merely by existing in this catalogue.

## Design intent

The seven-continent atlas remains canon. This expansion adds named places that give the existing regional blueprint more strategic depth without pretending every continent is already explorable. The catalogue separates three things that must never be conflated:

- **Discovery:** the party finds a place and records it in the atlas.
- **Clearance:** a dungeon threat is resolved and its route, evidence or resource becomes available.
- **Sovereignty:** a castle, town or strategic site changes hands through siege, negotiation, charter or restoration.

A dungeon clear must never silently transfer ownership of a province. Likewise, defeating a garrison does not automatically make a town loyal.

## Capturable places

The catalogue adds 17 authored strategic locations across the seven-continent setting. Each has a local identity, existing owner, capture method, economic profile, garrison tier and governance model.

### Eldrimar — The Crown Continent

- **Sallow Ford Keep (Sethmarch):** a flooded border castle controlling the Sallow ford and the western supply road. Its castellan claims a dead king's trust; the garrison is unpaid. Occupation can open a protected supply route, but the river's seasonal flooding remains a logistical constraint.
- **Foxglove Crossing (Orrowen Weald):** a market town of herbalists, charcoal burners and bowyers. Its elders demand that coppice boundaries and hunting rights be recognised before accepting a new lord.
- **Elderbough Watch (Orrowen Weald):** a tree-grown frontier fort whose signal bell covers hidden forest paths. It rewards stewardship and patrol investment, not indiscriminate logging.
- **Redwater Granary (Vessmoor Plains):** a walled grain town whose communal storehouses can feed a field army. Requisitioning grain may win a short campaign and lose the freeholders for a generation.
- **Milefort (Vessmoor Plains):** a plain but defensible road fort at an imperial mile marker. Its toll income and position make it a natural army-movement hub.
- **Glassmere Quay (Quillon Heights):** a lake port of glassmakers and lens-grinders. The Collegium bargains over trade but protects its archives.
- **Tarnholt Gatehouse (Tarnholt Cloisters):** a fortified hospice on the pilgrim road. Its infirmary, refugee sanctuary and relic custody are as important as its walls.
- **Ironvein Hold (Brennhallow Highlands):** a basalt mine-castle above the main iron seam. Its thanes judge rulers by miner safety and the fairness of ore shares.
- **Crownmarket Ward (Aurelmont):** a dense city district controlled through guild keys, the watch and civic legitimacy. It is a political objective, not a normal field siege.

### Thalassar — The Shattered Isles

- **Saltmere Harbour (Brinegate Coast):** a sheltered port with shipwright yards and a defensive harbour chain. Civilian shipping and pilot rights must be protected.
- **Veyhaven Beacon (Isles of Vey):** a cliff-top signal fortress. Restoring the beacon is part of claiming it; a captured dark lighthouse is strategically useless.
- **Morrowtide Exchange (Kelpward Reach):** a stilt-built salvage and medicine market governed by diver guilds and cargo ledgers.
- **Last Beacon (Deepwake Reach):** the final lighthouse before the black currents. Its lamp, oil cistern and keeper's stair must be restored before it can be claimed.

### Other continents

- **Redglass Wells (Varakhan):** an oasis town whose buried cistern keys and water-share laws are its true defences.
- **Ashwatch Citadel (Nhalor):** a half-buried volcanic castle that must be cleared, supplied with clean water and garrisoned before settlement.
- **Veyrwood Mootstead (Veyrwood):** a forest-ring settlement whose rotating moot cannot be replaced by a single imposed lord.
- **Skyhold of Asterreach (Asterreach):** a terraced pass-fort whose winter stores and signal mirrors protect isolated valleys.
- **Tidecourt of Ilyr (Ilyr):** an island city whose fleet obeys harbour law and treaty legitimacy, not simply the strongest garrison.

### Dungeon quality rules

1. Every dungeon has a region, recommended tier, floor count, distinct type, authored description, mechanics, named boss, rewards and a persistent world consequence.
2. Bosses should test the dungeon's central mechanic rather than merely have larger health pools.
3. Rewards should include evidence, route knowledge, civic resources or faction leverage as well as equipment.
4. A failed attempt should preserve discovered shortcuts and solved permanent mechanisms where appropriate.
5. Dungeons may be revisited for faction activity, optional contracts or ecology-specific encounters, but unique evidence and one-time relics cannot be farmed.

## Capture and administration loop

1. **Reconnaissance:** learn the garrison, local owner, supply access, civilian needs and political claim.
2. **Approach:** choose siege, negotiated charter, local compact, beacon restoration or political control as authored for that site.
3. **Resolution:** resolve the tactical objective or validated agreement. A breached gate is not the same as lawful occupation.
4. **Occupation:** assign a governor/castellan and decide the initial garrison, tax and local charter.
5. **Stabilisation:** address food, safety, law, trade and local custom. Neglect can produce unrest, sabotage, smuggling or a rival claimant.
6. **Integration:** connect the location to a supply route and regional market. Only connected holdings contribute full strategic value.

## Map expansion and production plan

The atlas is the global layer; each playable region still needs a bounded authored map with terrain, landmarks, encounters, exits and settlement interiors. The next map-production slices should be:

1. Sethmarch: Sallow Ford Keep approach, Foxglove road fork and a lower Sallow marsh loop.
2. Orrowen: Elderbough Watch and the Hollowroot/Warden's Barrow wilderness.
3. Vessmoor: Redwater Granary and Milefort road network.
4. Brinegate: Saltmere harbour approaches and the outer reef route.
5. Then unlock one playable region at a time on Varakhan, Nhalor, Veyrwood, Asterreach and Ilyr.

This file is a data and narrative foundation, not a claim that all 18 dungeons, 17 holdings or all seven continents are currently playable. Each site still needs a real map, encounter pool, interaction hooks, battle definitions and tests before it can be marked playable.
