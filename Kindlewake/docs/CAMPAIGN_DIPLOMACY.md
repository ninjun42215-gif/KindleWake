# Campaign Diplomacy Ledger

The Global Atlas exposes a diplomacy ledger for House Dunmarrow. It summarizes each faction's current relation band, treaty state, recognized territory holdings and recent campaign history.

Standing is a presentation of the existing -100 to +100 relation score:
- Hostile: -50 or lower
- Strained: -20 to -49
- Neutral: -19 to +19
- Cordial: +20 to +49
- Trusted: +50 or higher

Treaties progress through three thresholds: non-aggression at 20 relation, trade pact at 35, and alliance at 50. The ledger offers the next treaty tier only; war blocks all proposals. Existing story-authored diplomatic state changes remain available to campaign events. The ledger does not manufacture diplomatic gains: relations still change through authored campaign events and story decisions. Territory transfers remain controlled by battle, treaty and narrative systems.

All data comes from the existing campaign faction state and atlas definitions. The UI is read-only apart from a valid alliance proposal and introduces no save-schema changes.
