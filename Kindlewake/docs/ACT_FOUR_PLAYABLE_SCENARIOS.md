# Act IV — Winter of Knives: Playable Implementation

## Canon and narrative intent
The Quillon Archive is a social and investigative institution first, with a break-in or siege only as an optional failure path. Edmer's concealed clause is morally ambiguous: it protected someone from an immediate injustice while weakening the public record. The witness who recanted retains agency; the company may investigate threats but does not compel testimony.

## Playable scenarios
1. **The Quillon Archive:** present verified evidence, petition custodians, seek coalition mediation, or force entry. The forced-entry branch has an optional tactical encounter with the Quillon Warden. Once the revision is recovered, choose to preserve the original, publish a verified copy with protected names withheld, or entrust custody to the coalition.
2. **The Witness Who Recanted:** honor the withdrawal, offer a safe haven subject to the family's consent, or investigate the threat without asking the witness to testify again.

## Companion roles
- Brother Wystan argues that the witness's first need is safety and agency, not usefulness to the campaign.
- Sela helps compare route evidence and the courier's movements.
- Bram's experience with the river retreat informs the discussion of orders and responsibility without forcing a reconciliation outcome.

## Persistent consequences
- Archive access route, breach state and custodian trust.
- Original preservation, verified-copy publication and coalition custody are separately recorded.
- Edmer's revision and the moral context of his concealed clause are recorded.
- Witness withdrawal, agency, threat evidence, relocation offer and consent requirement are recorded independently.
- chapter_4_complete is set only after the witness scenario is resolved.

## Validation limits
JSON and map structures can be checked statically. Godot parser, headless suite and in-engine playtesting remain required. The Warden battle is an optional commander-defeat encounter, not a mandatory gate.
